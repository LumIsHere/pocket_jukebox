Pocket Jukebox is a Minecraft Java Edition mod inspired by Minecraft Bedrock Edition's built-in music feature add-on, bringing a convenient way to enjoy your favourite music discs wherever you go!

🎶 Play Music Anywhere

Introducing the Pocket Jukebox — a portable way to enjoy music in your Minecraft world. No need to stay next to a jukebox anymore! Take your music with you and listen to your favourite discs wherever your adventures lead.

![Creative Mode Inventory](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/2e1b69fcb9c33d401d0ebc4d2e6f2c3c8820452f.png)

### Blank Discs
The blank disc is only used for crafting, they can't be made into a playable music disc
